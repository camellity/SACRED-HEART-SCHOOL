SACRED HEART SCHOOL

School Information

Sacred Heart Co-Ed Sr. Sec. School is a CBSE-affiliated co-educational institution in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, established in 2008. It offers education from Nursery to Senior Secondary (12th Standard) in the English medium. The school is located Near Lakhani Square, Mundla Nayatha, on the Bypass Road, Dhar Road, Indore (Pin: 452002). It holds a customer rating of 4.3/5 based on over 120 reviews. Parents and students frequently praise the friendly environment and supportive teachers, while some reviews highlight it as a high-level school suitable for comprehensive schooling from Class IV to XII. Operating hours typically run from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM, Monday through Saturday.

Principal's Message

Sr. Delphy (PHJC)

Principal

Dear All,

The new session has already begun… some new faces with lots of smile and sparkling eyes … many old faces with new glitters of hope after two years of uncertainty… that is what I see these days in the campus. As I wish all of you God's blessings, just want to remind you about what makes life go on… It is wisely said “Life doesn't stop because something happens to you. We are born to keep moving and never quit. Plan your days and fulfill them.”

Set a goal… a goal for your life. Rhonda Byrne, in her book The Secret, says that the Universe will align itself to help you fulfill your goal. Nicholas Vujicic, an author and motivational speaker, is a shining example of a person who realizes his goals. He was born with tetra-Amelia syndrome, a rare disorder characterized by the absence of arms and legs. Doctors said that he will not be able to walk. At 21, he graduated with double degree in accounting and finance. He is the president of two companies and successfully runs four NGO's that reach out to thousands of people.

As cliché as it sounds, “Where there is a will there is a way.” Such living legends become inspiration when we feel low… therefore frame a goal, write it down, visualize it, create a positive mental picture of it, and talk about it to close friends and work hard to make it a reality. Trust in God, believe in yourself—that will improve your chances at success. And even if you do fail when pursuing your goals, the lesson learned will become a great learning experience.

With Best Wishes and God's Blessings,
Sr. Delphy (PHJC)
Principal
