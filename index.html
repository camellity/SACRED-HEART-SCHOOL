SACRED HEART SCHOOL

School Information :

Sacred Heart Co-Ed Sr. Sec. School is a CBSE-affiliated co-educational institution in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, established in 2008. It offers education from Nursery to Senior Secondary (12th Standard) in the English medium. The school is located Near Lakhani Square, Mundla Nayatha, on the Bypass Road, Dhar Road, Indore (Pin: 452002). It holds a customer rating of 4.3/5 based on over 120 reviews. Parents and students frequently praise the friendly environment and supportive teachers, while some reviews highlight it as a high-level school suitable for comprehensive schooling from Class IV to XII. Operating hours typically run from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM, Monday through Saturday.
