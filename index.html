SACRED HEART SCHOOL

School Information :

Sacred Heart Co-Ed Sr. Sec. School is a CBSE-affiliated co-educational institution in Indore, established in 2008, offering education from Nursery to Higher Secondary level. The school uses English as the medium of instruction and is located near Lakhani Square on Bye Pass Road, Dhar Road (Mundla Nayatha, Indore, MP 452002)
